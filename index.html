How it works

Daily habits — tap the big circle on a card to check the habit off for today; tap again to undo. The flame shows your current streak: consecutive scheduled days completed. Today doesn't break the streak until it's over.

Weekly-target habits — for things like working out, where it's the count that matters, not the day: set a target such as 3× per week and check days off whenever they happen. The card's ratio shows this week (e.g. 2/3) and the streak counts weeks in which you hit the target; the running week never breaks the streak until it's over.

Start/stop habits (intermittent fasting) — the same button does both: tap in the evening to start the fast, and tap when you eat again. While fasting, the card shows the live elapsed time; afterwards it shows how long your last fast was. A fast counts for the day it ends on. Set a fasting goal when editing the habit: a ring fills around the button as the fast progresses like a traffic light — red for the first third of the goal, yellow for the middle third, green for the home stretch — while the card counts down how long is left and shows the clock time (24-hour) at which you'll hit the goal (e.g. “2:30 left, goal 11:30”). Everything turns green when you reach it.

Editing a fast — forgot to press start or stop on time? In the habit's detail view, tap any fast in the list (including the one in progress) to correct its start and end times, or delete it.

Week dots — the M–S dots show this week (Monday first); filled dots are completed days, dimmed dots are days the habit isn't scheduled, and the ratio (e.g. 5/7) is completed / scheduled this week.

Fixing the past — in a habit's detail view, the month calendar is editable. For daily and weekly habits, tapping a day cycles it: done → skip day → empty. A skip day (shown hollow with a dashed outline) is excused — it neither breaks nor extends a streak, and it leaves the completion count — perfect for holidays or sick days. For fasting, tapping an empty day asks how long the fast was (entering 0 marks a skip day); tapping a filled day removes that day's fast. Use the arrows to move back through months; future days are locked. Streaks and stats update immediately.

Day notes — tap the pencil in the calendar header to enter note mode, then tap any day to write a short note (“skipped, travel day”, “hard fast”). Days with notes get a mark, and the month's notes are listed under the calendar; tap one to edit it.

Tonight's goal — press-and-hold the fasting habit's start button to pick a goal for just this fast (13/15/16/18h or the habit's default) before it starts. You can also change the running fast's goal by tapping it in the detail list. The ring, countdown, and goal time follow the per-fast goal.

Details — tap anywhere else on a card for the year-by-year pixel grid, current and longest streak, and completion %. Small letters mark where each month begins, and tapping any pixel shows that day's date, status, and note. Timer habits also list recent fasts with average and longest duration.

Undo — starting or ending a fast, and removing entries, can be taken back for a few seconds via the Undo bar at the bottom of the screen, so a stray tap never costs you anything.

Reorder, layout & theme — Settings → Reorder habits moves habits up and down the Today list; Settings → Layout switches between the list view and a two-column grid; Settings → Theme picks auto (follow the system), always light, or always dark.

Icon badge — on an installed app, the home-screen icon shows how many scheduled habits are still open today. It updates whenever the app is opened or used (a web app can't change it in the background).

Archiving — done with a habit but want to keep its history? Archive it from its detail view: it disappears from Today but keeps everything, and can be restored any time from Settings → Archived habits. Deleting, by contrast, is forever.

Adding & editing — ＋ creates a habit with a name, icon, color, and type; the icon picker offers 40 hand-drawn icons and a search field that understands English and German words (try “kaffee” or “sleep”). Daily habits can be limited to certain weekdays. The pencil in the detail view edits it (the type is fixed once created).

Stats — the chart button on the Today screen opens an overview of all habits: current and best streak, this month vs last month, and the last 30 days as a pixel strip per habit.

Milestones — hitting a round streak (7, 14, 30, 50, 100… days, or 4, 10, 26, 52 weeks) or setting a personal-best fast earns a short celebration. Each milestone fires once.

Fast chart — the fasting detail view charts your recent fasts as bars against the dashed goal line, so the trend is visible at a glance; tap a bar to edit that fast.

Your data — everything is stored only on this device. Export opens the share sheet on iPhone, so the backup can go straight to iCloud Drive (elsewhere it downloads a file); Import restores it. Settings shows how long ago the last backup was — since the phone holds the only copy, an occasional backup is worth it — and the app asks the system to protect its storage from cleanup (the status is shown below). “Export CSV for Excel” shares two spreadsheet-ready files (semicolon-separated, decimal commas): one row per logged day with status and note, and one row per fast with start, end, and duration.

Traffic-light ring.

  • Progress ring with phase colors — the fasting ring is now thicker and changes color with progress toward the goal: red for the first third, yellow for the middle third, green for the last stretch and beyond. One glance at the ring now tells you roughly how deep into the fast you are.

An icon for everything.

  • 24 new hand-drawn icons — the habit icon set grows from 16 to 40: heart, heartbeat, music, sleep, medication, water glass, coffee, less-screen-time, quit-smoking, alcohol-free, leaf, walking shoe, swimming, kettlebell, idea bulb, camera, paint brush, saving (€), pet paw, flower, mountain, conversation, cold shower (snowflake), and stairs — all in the same wobbly ink style.
  • Icon search — a search field above the icon picker filters by name and keywords in both English and German (“kaffee”, “schlafen”, “rauchen”, “swim”…), so the right icon is three letters away.

v1.8.1 — 31 Aug 2026

More habits per screen.

  • Compact cards — the Today panels are about a quarter shorter: tighter padding and gaps, a slightly smaller button, ring, and type, with every element still present. Roughly two more habits now fit on screen before scrolling.

v1.8 — 31 Aug 2026

Polish & protection.

  • Every pixel answers — tapping a day in the year grid shows its date, status (done / skip / not done), and note in a toast; skip days now render hollow in the year grid too.
  • Month markers — small letters in the year grid mark where each month begins, so a specific week is findable in the 365 pixels.
  • App icon badge — the installed app's icon shows the number of scheduled habits still open today (a running fast counts as underway, not open). Updates whenever the app is used; the count clears when everything is done.
  • Theme choice — Settings → Theme cycles auto / light / dark, for those who want the app permanently dark (or light) regardless of the system setting.
  • Storage protection — the app requests persistent storage from the system so habit data can't be silently evicted, and Settings shows the protection status and current data size.

v1.7 — 30 Aug 2026

The analysis layer.

  • Stats overview — a new chart button on the Today screen opens a per-habit overview: current streak, best streak, this month vs last month (completion % for daily and fasting habits, check-off count for weekly targets), and the last 30 days as a mini pixel strip with skip days hollow. Archived habits stay out of it.
  • CSV export for Excel — Settings → “Export CSV for Excel” produces two files via the share sheet (or as downloads): days.csv with one row per logged day (date; habit; type; done/skip; note) and fasts.csv with one row per fast (habit; start; end; duration in hours). Semicolon-separated with decimal commas, so they open cleanly in German/Austrian Excel.

v1.6 — 30 Aug 2026

Life happens.

Skip days show as hollow dashed circles (and hollow week dots), don't break streaks, don't count as completed, and leave the completion denominator — so a holiday no longer wipes out a 100-day streak. For weekly targets, a week containing a skip day is excused when the target wasn't met.
  • Per-fast goal — press-and-hold the start button to choose tonight's goal (13/15/16/18h or the default) before the fast begins, or change the running fast's goal from its entry in the detail list. Ring, countdown, and goal time all follow it; the habit's default stays unchanged.
  • Day notes — a pencil toggle in the calendar header switches to note mode: tap a day to write a short note, marked on the day and listed under the calendar for that month. Notes are included in backups.

v1.0 — 30 Aug 2026

Initial release of AMS Tracking.

  • Today overview — all habits as colored cards in one scrolling area: emoji icon, habit color, check circle, current streak with a flame, and Monday-first week dots with a completed/scheduled ratio.
  • Daily habit type — one tap checks off today, one tap undoes it; optional weekday scheduling (e.g. Mon/Wed/Fri only) where off-days neither count nor break streaks.
  • Start/stop timer habit type — built for intermittent fasting: a single toggle button starts the fast in the evening and stops it when eating again; the card shows the live elapsed time (h:mm) while running and the last fast's duration when stopped. An "Intermittent Fasting" habit is pre-created on first launch.
  • Fast history — the detail view lists the last 10 fasts with date and duration, plus average, longest, and total count; individual fasts can be deleted. Accidental stops under 5 minutes require confirmation.
  • Pixel year view — a "365 days, pixel by pixel" grid per year (all years with data), with current streak, longest streak, and completion percentage.
  • Habit management — create and edit habits with 16 emoji icons and an 8-color palette; delete with confirmation removes the habit and its history.
  • Backup — export all data as a JSON file and import it back, with a confirmation before anything is replaced.
  • App platform — installable PWA with generated icons, offline via service worker (network-first pages so updates arrive), automatic light/dark mode, and all data kept in local storage on the device — no servers, no accounts.
